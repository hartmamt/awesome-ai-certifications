# Contributing

Corrections are the most valuable contribution to this list. Certification details change without
notice, and a stale figure is worse than no figure.

## Correcting a fact

Open an issue or a PR with a link to the vendor page that contradicts what is here. That is all
that is needed — no formatting work required for a correction.

## Adding a certification

- One pull request per certification.
- Format: `- [Name](official-url) - Description.` Start with a capital, end with a period.
- **Link the vendor's own page**, not a training provider's summary of it.
- Include the figures that make the entry useful: number of items, time, cost, validity period,
  and whether anyone can register. If a figure is not published, leave it out rather than guessing.
- The certification must be currently offered, or clearly marked as retired with its retirement date.

## Adding a study resource

- Free official material is preferred and listed first.
- Paid resources are acceptable if they are genuinely useful, but say so plainly.
- **Disclose any affiliation.** If it is yours, say so in both the entry and the pull request.
  Undisclosed self-promotion will be removed.
- Exam dumps, brain dumps, and recalled live exam questions will not be accepted. They violate
  every vendor's candidate agreement and put the person using them at risk of decertification.

## Style

- Keep descriptions short and factual. This is a reference, not a review site.
- No affiliate or referral links, anywhere.
- Check spelling and grammar, and strip trailing whitespace.
